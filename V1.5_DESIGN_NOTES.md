# v1.5 Design Notes

The P&ID was rebuilt from the agreed reference image rather than extending the old v1.4 drawing.

## Fixed topology
- 4 PORT tanks and 4 STBD tanks; one reversible valve per tank.
- Independent PORT and STBD ballast mains.
- HIGH SEA CHEST and LOW SEA CHEST each have one isolation valve and connect directly to the COMMON SUCTION HEADER. There is no sea header.
- COMMON SUCTION HEADER has one suction isolation valve for each of BP1/BP2/BP3.
- COMMON DISCHARGE HEADER has one discharge isolation/throttling valve for each of BP1/BP2/BP3.
- BP1 is VFD controlled from 30–100%; BP2/BP3 are fixed speed.
- Pump branches include NRV indication on discharge side.
- V-P-SUC / V-S-SUC are placed physically on the side-main-to-common-suction branches.
- V-P-DIS / V-S-DIS are placed physically on the common-discharge-to-side-main branches.
- One straight vertical side bypass is drawn on PORT and one on STBD; each bypass has exactly one valve (V-X-P / V-X-S).
- Overboard is a separate branch from COMMON DISCHARGE HEADER.

## Drawing rule
A white junction dot means a real hydraulic connection. A crossing without a dot must not be treated as connected. Pipe animation is generated only for the route segments that belong to a complete hydraulic path.

## Training functions preserved/added
- Sea → tank pumping
- Tank → overboard pumping
- PORT → STBD and STBD → PORT pumped transfer
- Gravity filling through a stopped centrifugal pump when both pump isolations are open and head is sufficient
- Gravity discharge through common suction when tank head exceeds sea head
- BP1 VFD speed control using simplified affinity-law behavior (Q proportional to speed, head proportional to speed², power/current tendency proportional to speed³)
- Heel, trim, draft, free-surface/GM, alarms, water-fill visualization, and source-to-sink flow animation
