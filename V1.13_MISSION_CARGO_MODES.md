# v1.13 — Free / Mission / Cargo Modes

- FREE: unrestricted ballast operation with alarms only.
- MISSION: 5-level training ladder with direct level selection, restart and SKIP for verification.
- CARGO: automatic virtual cargo loading. Cargo mass changes displacement, trim and heel; trainee counteracts it with ballast.
- Level 1: single tank level target.
- Level 2: multiple tank targets.
- Level 3: ship-condition target.
- Level 4: main deballast to suction limit, then eductor stripping.
- Level 5: ballast-plan challenge with only final ship condition specified.

Cargo geometry/rates and mission tolerances are training parameters, not vessel-certified values.
