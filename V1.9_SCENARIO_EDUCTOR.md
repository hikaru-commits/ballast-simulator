# v1.9 Scenario / Eductor training upgrade

Based on the supplied ballast-operation workshop sheets and sample ballast line figure.

## Added
- Scenario Mission panel: WORKSHOP 01 — No.4 WBT Deballast
- Workshop initial state: No.4 WBT(P) 10.0 m, No.4 WBT(S) 8.0 m, valves closed
- 8-step assisted mission flow with optional hints and progress indicator
- Eductor No.1 on the P&ID
- V-WER1 motive-water valve and V-WES1 suction valve
- Simplified Venturi model: motive pressure/flow, negative suction pressure, suction flow
- Training warnings when suction is opened before vacuum or motive pressure is insufficient
- Eductor status card on the right panel

## Important
The supplied workshop itself states that it is a workshop specification and may differ from actual ship operation. This simulator upgrade follows the educational logic and valve terminology as a training model; it is not an approved ship-specific operating procedure.
