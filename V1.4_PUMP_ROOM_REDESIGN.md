# v1.4 Pump Room Redesign

- Ballast pumps reduced from 3 to 2.
- One common suction header and one common discharge header.
- PORT MAIN and STBD MAIN each have a dedicated suction selector and discharge selector.
- One common sea chest with independent suction-header and discharge-header/gravity isolation valves.
- One overboard valve from the common discharge header.
- PORT→STBD and STBD→PORT tank transfer is supported through the pump manifold.
- Ballasting, deballasting, gravity filling and gravity discharge remain supported.
- Conflicting line-ups are detected and actual flow animation is suppressed.
- Pump-room P&ID is redrawn as three clearly separated functional blocks.
